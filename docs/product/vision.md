# Product Vision

## Current posture

EchLub is an experimental music-systems foundation and evidence lab. It does not currently have an accepted product thesis.

The repository explores three technical areas:

- **Performance plane:** audio, timing, transport measurement, and evidence.
- **Composition plane:** explicit musical document state and deterministic semantic operations.
- **Session fabric:** identity, epoch, capability negotiation, and checkpoints.

The composition foundation and exploratory WebRTC performance baseline are implemented to bounded scopes. They are technical evidence, not proof of a product direction.

## Positioning decision

Collaborative composition is not a current EchLub selling point or differentiator.

The collaboration-led demo direction failed to make collaboration perceptible: multi-cursor activity and scripted workflow transitions did not show musicians changing one another's work or creating a result that could not be understood as sequential automation.

A narrower musical proposal/review idea may remain a research question, but it is not a public headline, architecture mandate, or validated user promise.

No replacement product direction is selected by this decision. No latency superiority, production readiness, complete-CRDT, or final-transport claim is demonstrated.
